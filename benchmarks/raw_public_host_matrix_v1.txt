[validation]
The workflow aggregator and an independent local rerun both observed exactly
four declared cells and eight passed sample manifests with unique run IDs, one
source commit, one runtime binary hash, the declared factors and workload, and
successful lifecycle teardown. The regenerated matrix manifest is
semantically identical to the retained artifact; its normalized SHA-256 is
also identical after accounting for Windows newline conversion.

Independent validation recomputed every sender and receiver log SHA-256,
checked both host binary hashes, zones, NTP flags, measurement boundaries and
trace names, and reparsed completion, feedback, drop, duplicate,
authentication, replay and injected-delay counters. Timed samples contained
forward and reverse injected delay; zero-delay samples reported zero injected
delay while retaining drops, duplicates and replay rejection. Primary and
independent cleanup manifests each covered all eight exact run IDs with empty
remaining-resource arrays. Authenticated GCP audits found no exact-run
resources after the workflow and no Aurora-prefixed Compute resources after
removal of historical network-only objects.

[interpretation]
All eight declared emulation lifecycles completed and cleaned up. The retained
numbers are descriptive observations of these eight runs. In both observed
region pairs, the timed profile had a higher mean receiver service interval
than the zero-delay profile; the magnitude is consistent with the declared
trace difference but this small, sequential campaign does not isolate a
causal effect. Differences between receiver zones are likewise descriptive
and must not be treated as a regional ranking.

[limitations]
There are only two sequential repetitions per cell. Run order was fixed,
public-Internet routing and cloud placement were not controlled, and the
independent application/controller clocks do not establish synchronized
one-way latency. Provisioning, package installation, compilation, SSH setup,
and teardown are outside the reported timing. No calibrated latency, goodput,
availability, energy, cost, regional, Internet-wide, physical-host, field, or
causal performance claim is made.
