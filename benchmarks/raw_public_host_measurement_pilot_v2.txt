[interpretation]
The campaign supplies descriptive within-cell variance estimates for the
declared primary and secondary same-clock metrics. The three primary-metric
standard deviations per cell range from 121.430 to 747.049 microseconds in
these observations. With only three observations per cell, those estimates
are themselves uncertain and are design inputs for a powered follow-up, not
evidence of a regional ranking or an injected-condition effect.

[limitations]
There are three observations per cell on one campaign day. Public-Internet
routing, GCP host placement and background load were not controlled. Blocking
reduces deterministic order imbalance but does not by itself identify a
causal effect. Independent application/controller clocks prohibit cross-clock
subtraction and one-way latency claims. Feedback RTT includes application,
declared impairment, network and polling service, so it is not network-only
latency. Provisioning, package installation, compilation, SSH setup and
teardown are excluded. No calibrated latency, goodput, availability, energy,
cost, regional, condition-effect, Internet-wide, physical-host, field or
causal performance claim is made.
